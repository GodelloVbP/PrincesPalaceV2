WHAT CHANGED

  • Cards keep the source's 3-state colour language (equipped / held elsewhere / free) but restyled into the game's painterly gold-rim-on-dark language instead of flat green/red/gold boxes.
  • "Held by X" now reads as a small badge in the card corner instead of parenthetical text buried in the description — scannable at a glance across a full relic list.
  • Clicking a relic held by another hero reassigns it to whoever's tab is active — no separate unequip step, matching the "one relic pool" rule.
  • Same hero-switcher component as the talent tree — the two screens should feel like one system.

FOR THE ENGINE

  • Relic pool is shared across the party (data model: one owner field per relic, not one list per hero). Clicking a card owned by hero B while viewing hero A reassigns it — a relic can only ever have 0 or 1 owner.
  • Card states: equipped (green→gold accent, "EQUIPPED" badge), held elsewhere (muted accent, "HELD BY {name}" badge), unclaimed (neutral gold border, no badge).
  • Icon is a placeholder gem/shard shape — swap for real relic art per-item; the ring/glow wrapper is state-driven chrome, independent of the icon art.
