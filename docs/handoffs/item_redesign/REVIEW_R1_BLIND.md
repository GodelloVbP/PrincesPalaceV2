Blind review of PLAN_ITEMS.md rev 1. Reviewer: Sonnet 5.5 (`implementer`, read-only), given only the plan and the repo, 2026-10-13.

## Verdict

Ready after fixes. The direction (chip-gated picks, per-member cards, one effect vocabulary behind the existing `HasRelic` seam) is sound and I1 is well-scoped. The reward loop has a dominated option (skip), a deterministic power track that gear replacement erases (honing), and an unspecified scaling story for the endless run. I2 is too large and depends on content (necklaces) that lands in I4.

## Findings

1. [blocker] Skip is a dead option. Sec 1.2 "Skip pays the shop sale price of the cheapest card" and "Take equips ... what it replaces goes to the bag". Taking the best card and selling the displaced or the new item yields at least the cheapest card's 30% sale (`ShopPricing.SellPrice`), and Take never costs a bag slot (no capacity, "Bag capacity" rejected). Scenario: the player takes every pick and sells whatever is worse; skip is never better except when every card is a downgrade AND take cannot decline to equip. The theory-score rows T1/T3 ("taking costs the skip gold") are false: taking costs nothing. Fix: either let Take choose "to bag" (then take-and-sell strictly dominates skip, so drop skip or make skip pay something take cannot, e.g. a flat per-tier gold), or pay skip the best-card sale price and make Take equip-only with the displaced item auto-sold at a lower rate. State the intended inequality as a test.

2. [blocker] Honing is erased by Take. Sec 1.5 + 1.2: honing is the only plus source, +1 per Rest or paid at the shop, but Take auto-equips and sends the old item (with its plus) to the bag. Scenario: the player hones a weapon to +4 at three Rests (giving up heals), the next Gear chip offers a tier+1 weapon, taking it throws away 3 Rests of investment, so the dominant play is "stop taking gear for slots I've honed" or "never hone until the last pick". Also Rest-hone is free in gold and a heal sacrifice only below full HP, with HP refilling only after the boss, so honing is nearly free when it matters least. Fix: carry plus onto the replacement (cap by new item), or hone only at the shop with a price that scales so a replaced item is a priced loss, and define the Rest tradeoff against a number (HP lost vs the value of +1).

3. [blocker] Endless scaling is unspecified and probably exponential. Sec 2.3 "budget formula unchanged", affixes `base x 1.25^tier`, tier = `step/16`, no cap past tier 10 (Q9 "keeps climbing"). 1.25^tier at tier 40 (realm 80) is about 7500x; `MaxPlus` 5 (+75%) becomes irrelevant after tier ~3, so hone/skip gold sinks die. Nothing says how enemy scaling compares, so "gear carries late power" may mean gear outruns enemies without bound or lags them. Fix: add a section giving the gear-power vs enemy-power ratio at steps 16, 80, 160, 320 as literals, and say what bends past the top tier (soft cap, flatter exponent). Gate I6 on that table.

4. [major] Tier, rarity and colour collide. Sec 1.2 "Quality = rarity = the colour. Common 0 affixes ... Epic 3; necklace Legendary". Code: `RarityBands.For(tier)` (`Domain/Content/Rarity.cs:36-56`) derives Rarity and colour from tier, with Mythic at tier 10, and `FloorTierOf` feeds reward floors. The plan never says what happens to that mapping, to Mythic, to the shop price/sort that read it, or to name adjectives. Scenario: implementer keeps `For(tier)` for names and adds quality colour, so a tier-8 Common is coloured Legendary or two colours disagree on one card. Fix: name `RarityBands` as replaced, define Mythic's fate, list the colour consumers (UI, shop, tooltip), and state that Legendary is reserved for necklaces (then affixed gear never exceeds Epic: is that intended at tier 30?).

5. [major] I2 is not a phase. It bundles base+tier asset collapse (638 assets to ~58), `rewards.json`, pipeline, per-member cards, pity, shop/shelf migration, evaluator, Reckoning UI and test rewrites (C7: 18+9+9 files), and it needs necklaces (pity, 1.3 trait precedence, `pool` column, `amulet` set listed in sec 2.4) that I4 builds. Pity cannot be tested in I2. Fix: split into I2a (base+tier ItemInstance, save fields, shop on pipeline, no behaviour change, same-seed stock pin), I2b (per-member cards, take-equips, skip, `Reward` stream), I2c (necklaces + pity, merged with I4). Give each a gate.

6. [major] Cards for a member may be meaningless or impossible. Sec 1.2: one card per member, slot drawn from that member's slots, traits "work only for wearer". Undefined: a trait that is useless for the recipient's kit (a spell-after-attack trait on a member with no spell; Odette vs Shawn), a member with no eligible slot after the `requirements` filter, a dead/benched member, squad >3, pick where the lowest-comparison member gets all spare cards. Scenario: a pick shows three cards, two for one member, one a trait the member cannot trigger; the "decision" is a non-decision. Fix: trait pool filtered by recipient kit (add a `requires` on trait rows), define the no-eligible-slot fallback and bench handling.

7. [major] Rules contradict each other on weapon and necklace priority. "An empty `Weapon1` wins outright" vs necklace pity weight vs "Boss: the lowest-weapon member's card is a weapon". Which wins when a boss pick hits an empty Weapon1 on one member, pity 3 on another? Also `VictoryRewards.GuaranteesWeapon(isBoss, isElite)` (`VictoryRewards.cs:166`, test pins elite=true) guarantees a weapon for elites too; the plan says boss only and makes anchors Gear OR Relic. Fix: a precedence list for card construction, and state what happens to the elite guarantee (anchors are the elites) and update the pinned test.

8. [major] Relic prize flow has holes. Sec 1.1: "Relic pool empty -> that Relic prize pays gold at the Skip value of 1.4" (1.4 is the axis table; skip value is in 1.2 and is a card sale price, so a Relic prize has no sale price to reuse). "The chip shows gold in that case, since the pool is known at realm open", but the pool shrinks during the realm as relics are taken (anchors, boss, gate, shop), so a chip fixed at realm open can promise a relic that is gone. Skip on a Relic or Book pick pays what? Boss now gives gear pick + relic pick + hub gate draft: up to 3 modals back to back. Fix: define relic skip value as a literal gold table by rarity, decide chip-resolution timing (re-evaluate at settlement and show the downgrade), cap modals.

9. [major] Book chip is undefined. Sec 1.1 "Book ... no choice": which spell, for whom, from what pool, what if the member already knows it or the pool is empty? The hidden `RollSpellDrop` is removed and replaced by a chip with no spec. T9 claims auto-pay but nothing says who learns it. Fix: spec the Book source row (pool, recipient policy, duplicate handling) in `rewards.json` or cut Book from I3.

10. [major] I1 gate cannot prove "no behaviour change" as written. "Bot batch ... matching a pre-I1 baseline within noise" gives no seed count, metric or tolerance; `tools/bot.ps1` exists but its offers/equipment are random, and the bot ignores gear value until I2 (Risk "bot blindness"), so the batch exercises relics only weakly. Moving headline numbers from `FightTuning` into JSON is a silent-change risk the plan itself names. Fix: name the metric and tolerance (for example identical per-fight final HP over N fixed seeds, exact match, since the refactor should be bit-identical), plus the literal-pinning test per moved constant as a named deliverable.

11. [major] Stacking rule conflicts with the rewrite. Sec 2.1: affixes "count once at best copy", stats "summed across holders". Trait + relic with the same stat kind: summed, but mechanic kinds a flag ("second copy does nothing"). 1.3 says a relic duplicating a worn trait "is offered", but the effect is then dead: a pick whose card does nothing for the wearer. Scenario: a relic pick offers a relic whose effect the party already has through a necklace; the "decision" is a trap. Fix: filter or flag it (strike dead cards), or define relic+trait duplicate as stacking magnitude for numeric mechanics.

12. [major] Claim "no saved item outlives a session" vs "necklacePity saved per run" and `RunSnapshot` changes. `RunManager.cs:65-80` says run-scoped prunes in `Reconcile` are "unreachable today". A save with old item ids still passes through load before settlement; the plan relies on Reconcile pruning unreachable code. Scenario: a crash on the pre-change build leaves `InventoryEntry` with old tier ids; the new build loads and settles it before Reconcile, hitting a null definition. Fix: add an explicit load test with an old-id snapshot, or bump the version and wipe run state.

13. [minor] Reward RNG determinism overstated. Sec 1.2 "Reckoning re-derives the same cards on reopen" but card content depends on current equipment, squad HP/benching and pity, all of which can change between roll and reopen (and Reckoning can be reached via debug menu). Fix: cache the rolled `Offer[]` on the run snapshot at settlement, or pin the claim to "same state".

14. [minor] Shop/gold loop unquantified. Hone price, sale value (30%), skip payout and shop floors are all "literals in I6". Nothing prevents gear-skip gold funding relic purchases (the 30% rule was designed so "selling the whole bag never funds a relic", `ShopPricing.cs:123`); skip pays exactly a sale price but fires every Gear chip, a new income stream. Fix: state a gold-per-realm budget and an invariant test (skip income over a realm < cheapest relic price).

15. [minor] Dangling references and unspecified literals. "Q7 Favor" shown "on the gear pick" and "squad Favor" feeding `LootLadder`: how squad Favor aggregates is undefined; "strength" floors need the run map's chip definition (cites `PLAN_RUN_MAP.md:91-102`, not self-contained); `pool` enum on affixes lacks the mirror-affix collapse mapping ("12 become 2... follows material": material to element table not given; which of the 25 become 15 isn't listed); `rewards.json` fields (`floorCheapestAtMost`, `qualityClass`, `stream`) have no definitions.
