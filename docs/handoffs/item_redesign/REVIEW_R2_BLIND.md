# Blind review of PLAN_ITEMS.md rev 2. Reviewer: Sonnet 5.5 (`implementer`, read-only), given only the plan and the repo, 2026-10-13.

## Verdict

**Ready after fixes.** The pick pipeline and settlement rules are mostly buildable, but the endless-run answer is "gear stops mattering at realm ~21 and Q9 will look at it later", and the skip/hone/gold loop has a dominated option and a price exploit. Several phases (I1 gate, I2a shop, I2b, I3) also leave literals or owners undefined.

## Findings

1. **[blocker] Reward power is flat for all of the endless run (1.6, Q9).** Tier caps at 10 (step 160) and honing at +10; the plan's own table has armour 9.3x against enemy ATK 391x and HP 1.06e5x at step 160, then enemy scaling caps at step 200 (`DifficultyCurve.MaxScaledStep`). After realm ~21 every gear pick is a sidegrade (quality only), every relic pick draws from an exhausting pool, and the Reckoning still opens at chip frequency. Scenario: realm 30, a boss pays four pages of picks that cannot change survival; the player learns to mash "skip the rest". The plan concedes this contradicts `OWNER_DECISIONS_2026-09-26.md:141` and defers to I5, after I2-I4 are built. The `DifficultyCurve.cs:200-205` comment says tier 10 is step 80; the code (`StepsPerTier` 16) says 160, and the plan's table follows the code. Fix: decide before I2a whether post-cap rewards exist (diminishing tier past 10, or relic-only/other currency past the cap). If gear is capped on purpose, stop issuing Gear chips past the cap instead of keeping the modal.

2. **[major] Skip is dominated by take (1.2, Q5).** Gold is spendable only at a shop (and honing), the bag is unbounded ("Bag capacity" rejected), and sale is 30% of the same `GearPrice`. Take equips the card, the displaced copy goes to the bag, and selling at the next shop yields at least the skip gold, with the item kept as an option. The plan's "skip is never dominated for gold" has it backwards: take is never dominated by skip. Skip pays 6g at tier 0 and 21g at tier 10 (`GearBase 21`, `GearPerTier 5`, sell 30%), up to about 53g with 3 affixes. Relic skip (18g, "cheapest card") is worse: relic cards cost nothing to take, so boss relic skip is never chosen. Scenario: a player who always takes and sells later loses nothing; skip is a dead option and "skip the rest" is only a time-saver. Fix: give skip something take cannot replicate (a depth-scaled sink such as a hone charge), or cap the bag so take costs a decision.

3. **[major] Hone price exploit (1.5).** Price is `BaseFor(tier of the slot's item) x (1 + 0.35 x plus)`, but plus lives on the slot and survives swaps and emptying. Scenario: leave a tier-0 item in `Weapon1`, buy +1 to +10 at 21g x (1 to 4.5), then take the tier-10 drop; honing the tier-10 item would have cost 71g per base. Undefined: price for an empty slot. A free Rest +1 can target any slot, so it always goes to the carry's `Weapon1` (weapon growth 1.35 against armour 1.25), a dominant choice. Fix: price on `FloorTier(step)` or `max(item tier, floor tier)`; state the empty-slot rule.

4. **[major] Gold has no deep sink (1.2, 1.5, section 5 "Gold").** Fight gold scales x1.075 a step (`DifficultyCurve.ScaleReward`), while `GearPrice` is linear, `RelicPrice` flat (60 to 700), hone linear. By step ~60 gold is effectively unlimited: every shop stock bought, every slot honed to +10, skip gold meaningless. The plan lists this as a risk and answers "skip stays at 30%", which does not address it. Fix: scale shop and hone prices with depth in I2a and state the gold-per-realm target I5 tunes toward.

5. **[major] Necklace pity and floors are under-specified and contradictory (1.2 steps 2, 3, 7, 8; 2.5).**
   - "Pity is read once at settlement for all picks in it and advances as each pick resolves": picks are rolled at once, so advancing cannot affect them. When does it advance, and does a necklace card rolled but not taken reset it? Reset-on-roll makes it free to take another card; reset-on-take means an unwanted necklace is never taken and pity grows without bound.
   - Pity increment, cap and the `lastPickBestCommon` reset rule are not stated; "chip strength 2/3" is not defined in this plan.
   - Weapon guarantee wants "one non-necklace card" for the owner with an empty or lowest `Weapon1`; if that owner holds the necklace card there is no rule.
   - Step 7 says quality "per card"; does a necklace consume a ladder roll, and is it the "best card" for skip value (priced as 3)?
   Scenario: two implementers produce different necklace rates and the I2b/I2c literals cannot be pinned. Fix: write pity as +1 per settled pick with no necklace card, reset when a necklace card is rolled, capped; state the necklace/weapon conflict rule.

6. **[major] Reckoning friction and card comparability (1.2).** One boss fight can queue boss gear, boss relic and two anchors: 4 pages of up to 3 cards. Each card shows one delta for a different member in a different slot, so cards are not comparable, and "take one" wastes two-thirds of what is generated. Take auto-equips on a fixed recipient, so a larger delta can hide a lost affix or trait. The plan scores T4 and T5 at 1. Scenario: players learn the carry's card is the only one that matters and mash "skip the rest" after page 1 (see 2). Fix: show affix/trait changes on the face when a slot is occupied, and cap a settlement at two pages by merging same-class picks, or measure card-pick time before I2b.

7. **[major] Shop tier, quality and hone under the new model are undefined (1.7, 2.2, 2.3, I2a).** 1.7 removes the tier ladder, the +-1 spread and the plus roll, and I2a pins shop stock as "same streams and counts", but nothing states the tier or quality of a shop copy, how `floorNoBare` ("at least Uncommon") maps to affix count, how the shop's quality draw relates to the pick ladder `p`, or how hone appears (a service row, not stock: price, reroll behaviour, stream). An I2a implementer must guess. Fix: add a shop paragraph to 1.2 (tier = `FloorTier`, quality = ladder with a named `p`, hone as a fixed row outside stock).

8. **[major] Gates and ownership gaps (section 4).**
   - I1: a byte-identical bot `RunTrace` only covers relics and affixes the bot meets; the source-scan test proves a read site exists, not behaviour. Require a literal test per moved headline number and the stacking test from section 5 in the gate itself.
   - I3: gate is `-Changed -BuildScenes` with no test area, yet it changes chip-to-pick mapping, anchor weights, boss picks, and removes the per-fight pick and hidden book roll. Add a test: Gear chip pays exactly one pick, other chips none.
   - Removals with no owning phase: plus roll, tier ladder and spread, `Fortunate`, `BossBonusPlusRungs`, rift tier, Mythic consumers (I2a lists only "storage").
   - I2b ships before I3: what pays picks in between? The per-fight pick is removed only in I3 and the chip context does not exist yet.
   - I3/I4 depend on run map P1/P2 landing `Relic` and the anchor weight list in a plan this one does not own; no fallback if P2 slips.

9. **[major] Effect model leaves double counting and stacking open (2.1, 1.3).** A trait excludes "a relic reaching them" only at roll time; a relic taken later grants the same mechanic and the trait silently adds nothing. "Affixes of one kind on one wearer count once" does not say whether a trait of that kind counts as an affix, and the pinned stacking test (two lifesteal affixes + Vampire Dentures) omits it. `EffectCatalog` records "tier-scaled or not" while `Magnitude` is a JSON number: which layer applies `1.25^tier`? Secondary numbers stay in `FightTuning`, so a new kind with its own numbers still needs a code file, undercutting "drops from 5 files to 3". Fix: one stacking table by holder pair, name where tier scaling is applied, and exclude or flag on the card a relic that duplicates a worn trait.

10. **[minor] `pendingPicks` storage (1.2, 2.5).** The plan says cards survive re-entry "in the same process", while `SettleOnOpening` ends any run found on slot entry, so persisting them buys nothing and grows the snapshot. Cards are "stored as `ShopStockEntry`" but a card carries a recipient and pick class, which `ShopStockEntry` (`ShopStock.cs:41-58`) lacks and 2.5 does not list. Fix: keep pending picks in memory only, or list `recipientId`, `pickKind`, `pickIndex`.

11. **[minor] Quality table and Favor (1.2 step 7).** Today's ladder is p 0.22 / 0.30 / 0.38 with 5 rungs (`LootLadder.cs`, `FavorPerPoint 0.006`, cap 0.55); the plan uses 0.40 / 0.50 / 0.40 with 3 rungs. Boss `p` is below the anchor's, so an elite's Epic share (12.5%) is close to a boss's (16%), and 12.5% of anchor picks are three Commons. Favor headroom is 0.05 at anchor (about 8 points to the cap) and 0.15 at normal, so Favor barely matters in the picks that matter. Fix: state the intended class ordering and Favor's useful range; lower anchor `p` or raise the cap.

12. **[minor] Rest is a free hone at full HP (1.5).** Heal-vs-hone is a choice only when hurt (HP refills at the boss per the plan); a healthy party gets the +1 free, and at +10 on every slot the option is dead. Fix: accept explicitly, or price the Rest hone.

13. **[minor] Undefined terms and literals.** "chip strength" (2/3), "heart" and "pocket" (run map terms), which `step` a heart-anchor or boss pick uses, `Weapon2` handling (absent from the weapon guarantee and slot weights; the non-necklace slots are not enumerated), whether `Quality` is derived or stored on bag and shop copies, card count when the squad is under 3 for relic picks, pity cap, empty-slot hone price, stream constant for shop and hone, steps per realm behind "realm ~21", and `skipPays: bestSale` when the best card is a necklace.

14. **[minor] Citation drift.** `HasRelic(` appears 40 times in 12 files under `Assets/_Project/Scripts`; the plan says 37 in 11 (the extras may be definitions). Re-count before sizing I1.
