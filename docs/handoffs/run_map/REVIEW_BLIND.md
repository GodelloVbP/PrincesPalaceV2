# Blind review of PLAN_RUN_MAP.md rev 3

Reviewer: Sonnet 5.5 (`implementer` agent, read-only), given only the plan text,
2026-10-13. Verbatim report below. Findings are unverified and unapplied: the
orchestrator triages them (see `README.md`).

## Verdict
Ready after fixes. Spine and pocket structure are sound, but the skip-heal/anchor economy has a dominant "focus the boss, ignore anchors" line, the strength-scaling rule can pay out nothing at the extremes, and several numbers and phase dependencies disagree.

## Findings

1. **[blocker]** §6, §2 pt 3, §11 — "The boss's death ends the fight" plus anchors that pay nothing unless killed makes anchors a skippable non-threat. Optimal play is to ignore every anchor and kill the boss first. Bossing is a single-target race; two anchors beside it only add incoming damage for a few turns, and the queue never even arrives if the boss dies first. The "queue as clock" only bites a party that kills slowly. So the pocket-anchor decision collapses to "take it for the shown prize, or skip it for free". Skipping costs almost nothing, and it earns the 8% heal as well. The plan itself flags this as an "open consequence" (§6) but gates only the measurement, not the design. Fix: decide the pressure before P3. Options: anchors that stand in front block the boss until one falls (Reach/front-rank), or each living anchor adds a boss buff or an extra boss action, or the boss only becomes targetable after N anchors fall. State the chosen rule in the plan, not in "P5 measures".

2. **[blocker]** §2 skip heal vs §4 table — 8% flat heal per skipped pocket, up to 6 pockets, so up to 48% HP free for walking the spine past mouths. At floor 10+ the spine route is "walk 8 rooms, heal 48%, face the boss with 6 anchors who contribute nothing". There is no HP carry-over cost for the spine, since HP already refills only after the boss. Always-skip then beats most loops unless loop prizes are large. It is also unclear whether the heal triggers per spine step that passes a pocket mouth, or only once per pocket. Fix: scale the heal down with pocket count (or cap total heal at about 20%), or make it a chosen action (rest at the mouth) so it is a decision, not a free tax-break on the dominant route. Also define whether it can overheal or happen at full HP (at full HP it is dead text and teaches nothing).

3. **[major]** §3 — pockets pay exp and loot at spine-level difficulty, and the plan admits that is power the curve doesn't see. Combined with scaled strength-3 rewards, a floor-100 player who takes all 6 loops (about 24-26 rooms) gets up to 3x the exp, gold and drops per realm at the same boss difficulty. "Always take every loop" is dominant for anyone who can survive, and the HP-carry penalty is weak when anchors are optional and bosses are single-target. This is deferred to the P0 EV sheet with no stated acceptance threshold. Fix: state target bounds in the plan (e.g. a full-loop run yields at most X% more power for Y% more HP loss), and name the lever (pocket-room difficulty offset, i.e. pocket rooms at offset+1 not offset(a)+...).

4. **[major]** §3 formula — pocket room offset `offset(a) + floor((j+1)*(offset(b)-offset(a))/(n+1))`, clamped to `offset(a)+1`. For a stretch where `offset(b)-offset(a)=1` (all adjacent spine nodes), every pocket room clamps to `offset(a)+1`, i.e. the same step as spine node b. A pocket of length 4 at floor 100 puts 4 rooms at one step. Strength, exp, loot all identical, the preview roll uses (step, nodeId) so enemies differ but the difficulty clock doesn't progress. Also "offsets never decrease along an edge" lets a pocket room land at offset(b) and then the next spine node also at offset(b): fine, but a 4-room pocket is then four fights at the same difficulty as the next spine room, i.e. flat difficulty for the whole detour. The longest route "24-26 rooms" at difficulty 8 steps range. Fix: say it is intended, and state exp/loot is flat (so the detour is "extra rooms at the same step"), or add a small per-room step bump for pocket rooms.

5. **[major]** §2 "strength scales steeply" vs degenerate cases. Strength is relative to the party as it entered the realm (1-3). At floor 1 a new party probably has no strength-3 fights (or all fights are 1-2), so "every pocket holds at least one prize ... or a strength-3 fight" (§2 end) is unsatisfiable and the relax order in §4 doesn't cover it. At floor 100, if enemy power grows with floor and "party as entered" tracks floor, strength may saturate at 3 for everything or 1 for everything depending on the formula, and nothing defines it. Since strength is frozen at realm entry and relative to the party, a party that grows steeply mid-realm (item, level) sees strength-3 nodes that are trivial, while rewards stay scaled by the stale label. Fix: define the strength function (inputs, thresholds, clamp) in the plan; define what happens when the profile's strength mix cannot be satisfied; make the "prize" guarantee exclude strength-3 for floors where none exist.

6. **[major]** §2/§6 — the anchor reward is paid "only if it dies, on the map or in the boss fight" yet the boss fight ends on the boss's death. Killing an anchor in the boss fight is strictly worse than on the map (boss hitting you) with the same prize, and the player who kills the boss first gets nothing. So the "kill it at the heart" option the plan advertises (§6 Rewards) is a trap; it should not be presented as a choice. Also the interaction with focus-fire: the optimal order is boss, then nothing. Fix: either anchors killed at the heart pay a bonus (price of risk), or drop the claim that it is a valid option.

7. **[major]** §6 vs §2 table — table says Elite (anchor) sits "pockets and forks"; §6 says "the two strongest living anchors take the other two [slots]" with ties by map order, but "strongest" is undefined for elites (strength 1-3 applies to fights; anchors have a "rolled enemies" roster and a strength?). §7 pips show strength for fights only. Also, the stage has 3 slots, but an anchor's "rolled enemies" may be a multi-enemy roster (§5, §6 "the boss roster takes the two strongest living anchors' rolled enemies" — plural). Does one anchor mean one enemy or its whole roster? If a roster, slots overflow. Fix: define anchor = exactly one elite enemy, and define "strongest" (strength label then map order).

8. **[major]** Phase ordering — P2 pays "reward chips" and P4 builds the screen, but §6 says "until P5, anchors are plain optional elites". P3's gate demands "anchors left alive must be reported with the boss-fight win rate they produce", which cannot exist before P5, and P3 precedes P5 (P5 reruns the gate, but P3's gate is unsatisfiable as written). Also P2 includes "a column projection so the current screen stays playable" with no spec of what it projects. And the P0 EV sheet needs pocket values and the strength multipliers before the generator exists, with the default-profile table undetermined at that point. Fix: split P3's gate: pocket-take and strength-take differ by band at P3, anchor win rate at P5. Specify the projection or drop it.

9. **[major]** §4 numbers vs §11/§4 table. Loops `1 + floor/2` ceiling 6 "reached at floor 10": 1 + 10/2 = 6, ok; but the floor table shows "Longest ~10 at floor 1", "~20 at floor 10", "~24-26 at floor 100". Spine 8 rooms + 6 pockets of up to +4 each = 8+24 = 32 rooms if all taken, and the spine has only 7 interior stretches (offset 0..8, "none bypassing offset 0 or 8" leaves stretches 1->2 ... 7->8 = 6 stretches; fine, six). So longest = 8 + 6*4 = 32, not 24-26 (unless pocket lengths average +3, or maximum is +4 only for some). Also "loop length +1-2 rooms ... widening to +1-4" and "Anchors: one per loop" at ceiling means 6 anchors plus forks' anchors; §11 says "no longer capped at 2". Fix: reconcile the table with the stated maxima; state whether the 24-26 is average or maximum.

10. **[major]** §2 Rest/Shop/Cache vs "Shops: 0 or 1 per realm, always inside a pocket". Shop only inside loops means a player who always skips loops never sees a shop and can never spend gold; gold then has no sink for the spine-only route, while Cache (banks gold) is also pocket-only. Pocket-skipping strategy hoards gold uselessly, and the pocket-taker is forced into shops with HP they paid. A shop chance below 100% also means a realm with no shop at all. Fix: allow a shop at a fork or an entry/heart-adjacent spine node at low rate, or give gold another sink so spine-only runs aren't gold-starved.

11. **[minor]** §2/§6 save and heart drawing — the heart draws "the boss, two beside it, and the queue", but anchors are "joined by a painted root" and withering is cosmetic; with 6 anchors at floor 10+ the heart and root layout of 6+ roots across a vertical map needs a defined cap on what is drawn; §7 says the queue shows "in order". Not buildable without a layout rule. Also: both orientations assumed, and the camera starts on the heart (2.5 s) which at floor 100 shows the tallest map; the heart is below, so the opening pans up from the bottom through the whole map every realm.

12. **[minor]** §8 migration — a v8 run at offset 5 is placed on the spine node at offset 5 "counted as cleared", with pockets "walked past without retroactive heal". A player mid-realm who had been on a fork path and used a rest loses nothing, but the new map may have an anchor-free spine with all anchors alive and the player cannot reach any pocket before offset 5. Acceptable, but then that realm's boss has anchors the player can no longer fight on the map, joining the boss fight unearned. Fix: mark anchors behind passed offsets as slain on migration.

## Questions an implementer would have to ask
1. Does the 8% skip heal fire once per pocket mouth passed, or once per spine step? Guess: once per pocket whose mouth node you leave via the spine edge, capped at max HP, and only if HP below max.
2. What is the strength formula? Guess: sum of enemy power / party power at realm entry, thresholds at 0.7 and 1.2, clamped 1-3.
3. Is an anchor one enemy or a full encounter roster? Guess: a single elite enemy.
4. What is "strongest" anchor for slot assignment? Guess: highest enemy power, ties by map order.
5. What if the profile's strength mix or "pocket has a prize" cannot be satisfied (floor 1 has no strength-3)? Guess: relax to the highest available strength and add a Treasure.
6. What is the column projection in P2? Guess: map spine offset to a column and pockets to a side column, show nothing for anchors.
7. Do anchors in the boss fight get the same reward chip payout when killed there? Guess: yes, paid after the fight like normal kills, boss still ends the fight.
8. Pocket length range per floor in the default profile (exact table)? Guess: floor 1: 1-2, floor 10: 1-3, floor 20+: 1-4, uniform.
9. Which spine stretches can host pockets and can two pockets overlap or nest? Guess: one per stretch, no overlap, no nesting.
10. Where do forks sit and what does a fork "branch" look like (equal-length branches between which spine nodes)? Guess: replace one spine node with 2-3 parallel nodes at the same offset.
11. How is the heal-at-boss-end ("refills only after the boss") affected by Rest nodes: how much does Rest heal? Guess: same as the existing rest.
12. What exactly is a "reinforcement at fraction of its HP" for anchors, and is the fraction visible to the player? Guess: bot-tuned constant, not shown.
