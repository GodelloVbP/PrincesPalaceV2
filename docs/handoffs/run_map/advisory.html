1

Routes of unequal length

The spine to the heart is always 8 steps, as today. Pockets branch off and rejoin, costing 1–2 extra rooms, and there are more of them each floor (1 at floor 1, up to 4). HP only refills after the boss, so a pocket is HP you carry into that fight. Each pocket holds something the spine lacks: an anchor, treasure, the cache or a strength-3 fight.

2

You see what you're walking into

A fight node shows the enemies it will spawn, strength 1–3 and one reward chip. Strength changes the kind of reward: gold, then gold plus a consumable, then a spell book. The preview uses the same roll as the fight, so it can't lie.

3

Anchors stand with the boss

The boss is drawn at the bottom from the start, with up to two anchors beside it. Each anchor is an elite out on the map, tied to the boss by a painted root. Beat it on the map and it's gone; leave it alive and it fights beside the boss. Elites are optional, and the boss drops one more reward for every anchor still standing. No counters, no percentages.

4

The realm eases (polish, last)

Found and eased paintings, with the eased one showing through around cleared rooms and around the boss as wards fall. Never labelled. Your decision spec asks for this.

On forks you pick which waypoint (shop or rest). On pockets you pick how much more you take on. On anchors you pick what boss you'll face.

What the reviews changed

Round 1

  • Dropped my partial heal between realms. The full heal is a deliberate fix for a measured attrition complaint.
  • Pinned down what step means. It is the difficulty clock for about 25 systems, so it needs a contract, not an audit.
  • Wards became specific boss abilities instead of a counter.
  • Four map templates cut to one, with parameters.
  • Art brief and your decisions moved to the front.

Round 2

  • The spine is fixed at 8 steps. A 5-room spine would have squeezed 8 steps of difficulty into 5 rooms.
  • The preview reuses the fight's own roll. A new rng stream would have let the preview disagree with the fight.
  • Wards split into their own phase after the bot gate. They need new combat code.
  • Added a reason to leave an anchor alive, plus a fixed node grammar.
  • Rejected one blind finding: the easing mask stays. Your spec asks for the world to respond.

Risks I'd watch

  • Pockets become a schedule. "Always when healthy, never when hurt" is a real risk. The P3 bot gate exists to catch it, and pocket prize size is the main lever.
  • It still looks like Slay the Spire. Structurally it is a node graph. The difference you'll feel is the boss changing shape because of your route. If the storyboards don't sell that, rethink before P1.
  • Boss plus two anchors could be brutal. Anchors join at a fraction of their HP, tuned by the bot.
