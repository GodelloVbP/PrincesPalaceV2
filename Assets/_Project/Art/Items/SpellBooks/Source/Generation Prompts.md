## Book master

Use case: precise-object-edit. Reference is style guide; generate ONE reusable closed spellbook icon centered square canvas, whole silhouette safe 8 percent margins. Painterly fantasy relic quality, broader shading with virtually no leather grain, compact thick tome, cover-dominant gentle three-quarter tilt clockwise 8 degrees, spine left, narrow cream pages bottom/right. Plain small neutral gunmetal corner guards and modest gray right clasp. Broad cover MUST BE saturated MAGENTA leather RGB around 200,40,200 with simple material shading, including magenta spine; this is a technical recoloring master. No gold or brown leather. Center round plate takes 48 percent cover width, very slim neutral gunmetal rim. Entire interior of plate perfectly flat CYAN #00FFFF circle (technical alpha placeholder), no shading texture icon or glyph inside that circle. Flat perfectly uniform GREEN #00FF00 background, no gradient, glow or cast shadow. Only background green; no green inside book. Only circle cyan. Only leather magenta. Gray hardware and cream pages MUST stay neutral. Simplify for 48x48 inventory icon, bold silhouette and rim. No text runes engravings decorative illustration seams or symbols.
